The project

A member experience
on mobile.

Working in a two-person team at ABC Cooking Studio, I helped build and launch a lesson booking app for members. The wider work included digitising booking forms and migrating the CMS from WordPress to Strapi.

Inside the app

Discover. Reserve.
Keep track.

  • A member dashboard with reserved lessons, waiting lists, and studio news.
  • A lesson catalogue organised by course category and studio.
  • Course history and credit information for members.
  • Lesson reservations from a mobile device.
