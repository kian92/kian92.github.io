FusionHome / AI products & e-commerce

See the possibilities.
Then make them real.

An AI-powered furniture store and a design SaaS platform, connecting room inspiration with practical tools and shopping.

Scope
AI tools · E-commerce · SaaS
Stack

The opportunity

Make a room easier
to imagine.

Furniture is easier to choose when you can picture it in your own space. FusionHome brings room visualisation into the shopping journey, while FusionHome Studio gives users a dedicated workspace for exploring interior ideas.

01 / Furniture store

Visualise before
you buy.

Customers upload a room photo and explore furniture placement with AI staging and object removal. The experience connects inspiration with a furniture store built on a customised Medusa platform.

I led development of the AI tools and customised the open-source e-commerce platform to support the store.

02 / Design SaaS

One room.
New possibilities.

FusionHome Studio brings AI staging and interior design into one workspace. Users can explore room styles, furniture, paint, walls and flooring, turn sketches into renders, and remove unwanted objects.

Technical delivery

The product behind
the visuals.

Alongside the AI workflows and storefront, I built credit-based subscriptions and revenue dashboards. I also developed AI agents for Feng Shui analysis and renovation cost estimation.

The work combined AI integrations, commerce, and the supporting tools needed to operate the products.
