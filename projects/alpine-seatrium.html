The challenge

Paperwork across
the whole rental cycle.

Bookings and maintenance were tracked on paper. The project brought those records into a shared system for office staff, field staff, and management.

What I built

One place to run
rental operations.

  • Bookings and equipment availability.
  • Customer contracts and invoicing.
  • Maintenance tracking, automated reminders, and service history.
  • An Android app for staff away from the office.
  • Migration of existing records into the new system.

The result

Less administration.
